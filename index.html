What Kolbo does

Log in with your CUIMS once. Kolbo reads your data and keeps it on your phone.

📊

Attendance + bunk calculator

See every subject's %, how many classes you can skip, and how many you must attend to reach 75%.

🗓️

Timetable with breaks

Today's classes in order, rooms, and your free slots. A heads-up 10 minutes before each class.

⏰

Reminders

"Remind me to submit DSA homework Friday at 5." Done. Add or delete them yourself too.

🎙️

Talk to it

Ask by voice or text: "Can I skip Maths tomorrow?", "When is my next exam?" — in English or Hinglish.

📝

Exams, marks, CGPA

Date sheet with countdowns, your marks and results in one place.

⏱️

Study timer + plans

Focus timer, daily study goal, and study plans that remind you when to start.

Is it safe?

Fair question. Here is exactly what happens.

🔒

Your password stays with you

Your CUIMS password is saved encrypted on your phone and is only typed into the official CUIMS site (students.cuchd.in).

⚠️

Why Android shows a warning

Kolbo isn't on the Play Store yet, so Android warns you like it does for any app shared as a file. That warning is normal.

🤖

How the AI answers

When you ask Kolbo something, your question and the numbers it needs (like a subject's attendance) go to Google's Gemini AI to write the answer.

📦

The file comes from GitHub

The button downloads the official release straight from GitHub. New versions come through an update popup inside the app.

Install in 1 minute

Steps can look a little different on each phone.

  1. Tap Download for Android above.The file is about 60 MB.
  2. Open kolbo.apk from your notifications or Downloads.
  3. If asked, allow Install unknown apps for your browser, then go back.This only lets your browser install this file. You can turn it off again after.
  4. Tap Install. If Play Protect pops up, tap More details → Install anyway.
  5. Open Kolbo and log in with your CUIMS ID.
